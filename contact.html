FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common inquiries regarding our work, donations, and governance.

Is Center for Dialogue and Research International Limited officially registered in Kenya?
Yes. Center for Dialogue and Research International Limited is legally registered in Kenya under the Companies Act with the Registrar of Companies (ROC) and adheres to statutory compliance under Kenyan law.
How do you ensure funds are actually used for community programs?
We operate with a strict financial transparency charter: 84% of all donations go straight to direct program implementation, 10% to rigorous monitoring & evaluation, and 6% to lean administration. We publish our annual audited accounts every financial year.
Can international donors donate to CLD Kenya?
Yes. International supporters can wire funds directly to our Equity Bank Kenya USD/EUR account (SWIFT: EQBLKENA) or use international debit/credit cards. We provide official charitable acknowledgment receipts for international tax filing.
Can communities or local leaders directly request CLD peace mediation during a dispute?
Yes. Through our 24/7 Peace Rapid Alert Hotline (+254 721 793 106) or the online inquiry form, community elders, chiefs, church/mosque leaders, or county commissioners can formally request rapid mediation intervention. Our certified Alternative Dispute Resolution (ADR) mediators deploy within 48 hours to assess the situation and initiate neutral dialogue barazas.
